DENTAL CLINIC FAQ - BRIGHT SMILE DENTAL

Q: What are your opening hours?
A: We are open Monday to Friday from 9am to 6pm, and Saturday from 9am to 2pm. We are closed on Sundays.

Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: You can book an appointment by calling us at 555-1234, using our website, or visiting the clinic in person.

Q: Do you accept insurance?
A: Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and BlueCross. Please bring your insurance card to your first visit.

Q: How much does a teeth cleaning cost?
A: A standard cleaning costs $80 for patients with insurance and $150 for patients without insurance.

Q: What should I do in a dental emergency?
A: Call our emergency line at 555-9999. We offer same-day emergency appointments for severe pain, broken teeth, or lost fillings.

Q: How often should I visit the dentist?
A: We recommend visiting every 6 months for a routine checkup and cleaning to maintain good oral health.

Q: Do you offer teeth whitening?
A: Yes, we offer both in-clinic whitening (1 hour session, $299) and take-home whitening kits ($99).

Q: Is there parking available?
A: Yes, we have free parking directly in front of the clinic with 20 spaces available.
